Privacy Policy

This explains, in plain words, what information the LaunchRiot Executive workspace handles and why.

1. What we access

Only what you choose to connect, and only with your permission: your email, your calendar and, if you connect it, your messages. You can connect or disconnect each one at any time in Settings. We do not access anything you have not connected.

2. How we use it

We use it to show you your own inbox and calendar in one place, to rank what needs your attention, and to suggest tasks and draft replies for you. Nothing is sent, replied to, deleted or changed on your behalf without your approval. Drafts stay drafts until you approve them.

3. We do not sell your data

We never sell, rent or trade your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train general-purpose AI models. People at LaunchRiot do not read your messages unless you ask us for support and give permission.

4. Each account is kept separate

Every workspace is isolated. Your data is not visible to other customers, and information from one account is never mixed into another.

5. Google user data (Limited Use)

LaunchRiot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In plain words: we use your Google data only to provide the features you see in your workspace; we do not transfer it to others except to provide those features, to follow the law, or with your consent; we do not use it for advertising; and humans do not read it except with your permission, for security, or to follow the law.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data only while your workspace is active. You can disconnect an account at any time, which stops all access. If you ask us to delete your data, we will delete it and confirm. Contact us using the details below.

7. Security

Access to your workspace needs your sign-in. Connections are made with your permission and can be revoked by you at any time.
